# Static planner-3 FIRI design

## Provenance and solver

GCOPTER firi.hpp, copyright Zhepei Wang (2021), upstream https://github.com/ZJU-FAST-Lab/GCOPTER, pinned e0444f6d47b84f972ced91746b05feb36ce1fd4f. FIRI_NOTICE preserves MIT attribution. No lbfgs.hpp/sdlp.hpp helper source is copied. Installed Eigen and NLopt supply linear algebra and SLSQP; package metadata declares libnlopt-dev.

This is a materially adapted 2D FIRI approach, not unchanged upstream. Restrictive separation now enumerates finite angular extrema, replacing the obsolete two-variable SLSQP solver and its positive-offset representation. For unit world normal u it maximizes min_v u.(v-center)/|L^T u| subject to u.(v-seed_i)>=clearance for all four cell vertices and both endpoints. The offset is min_v u.v-clearance, without a sign restriction. One plane excludes the entire convex cell. On each cell support sector the objective is linear divided by an ellipsoidal norm: extrema occur at +/- (L L^T)^-1(v-center), support-switch directions, or clearance-active directions satisfying u.(v-seed_i)=clearance. Enumerating those boundaries and stationary points is complete in real arithmetic; floating results still require the unchanged normalized metric certificates. Empty feasible candidates report REFERENCE_OCCUPIED, nonfinite computations NUMERICAL_FAILURE, and expired deadlines BUDGET. No retries, angular sampling, relaxed certificates or optimizer status acceptance is involved.

For positive support this is the former restrictive objective expressed without division by a potentially zero/signed offset: maximizing support/ellipsoidal norm equals minimizing the rescaled normal norm. Negative support remains representable, rather than being mislabeled infeasible. Clearance is subtracted after support selection, as before; this does not claim global optimization of the clearance-shifted ellipse or polygon volume. The alternating certified NLopt MVIE step and bounds remain unchanged. Its initialization still requires a strictly interior center; a standalone feasible separator with a center inside the obstacle does not promise a complete inflation can retain that center. Upstream firi.hpp lines 307–333 instead construct 3D point tangents and project them against seed endpoints (with a cross-product final case); dropping in point/corner tangents would not certify whole cell interiors. No upstream helper source was copied.

Geometric separation has no optimizer status or initialization. NLopt applies only to MVIE: success/FTOL/XTOL still require finite values and independent constraints. The existing MVIE-only ROUNDOFF_LIMITED exception may supply a candidate to the unchanged positive-diagonal, containment and shrink certificates; it is not solver success. Max evaluations, timeout, other exceptions and failed certificates reject and clear output. Every inflation cycle computes MVIE. Iterations are bounded, not a claim of global convergence or maximum polygon volume. Nearest-vertex ordering can affect region quality.

## Geometry

Optimize and certify in seed-local coordinates. Retain normalized world halfspaces for existing MPPI queries and certified polygon vertices for visualization. Whole-cell separation, seed containment, bounded domain, positive polygon area and ellipse containment are checked. Unknown/nonzero mask entries block. Intersections are rebased locally; area subtracts its first vertex before products.

After original-edge generation, run one deterministic GCOPTER shortCut-style backward earliest-predecessor pass. Skip middle regions only when the retained pair has the existing >=1e-6 m interior witness and its union continuously covers the intervening original path, analytically clipping each original edge against normalized planes (existing 1e-8 m certificate tolerance, no interval-gap tolerance). Retain first/last regions and original s0/s1 seed metadata; metadata gaps do not imply geometric gaps. Recheck full original-path coverage and every retained join, recomputing overlap diagnostics under the same cooperative deadline. This is not minimal-cardinality optimization; raw generation still consumes the unchanged region cap before pruning.

Numerical envelope: absolute map/path coordinates <=10000 m; resolution >=0.01 m; grid span <=100 m; local radius [0.01,100] m; clearance/progress/overlap depth >=1e-4 m; area threshold >=1e-8 m^2. Plane certificates use 1e-7/1e-8 m tolerances; intersection determinant cutoff 1e-10. Bounds are safeguards, not exact predicates or universal convergence guarantees. Defaults: radius 3 m, clearance .002 m, 4 inflation passes, 150 optimizer evaluations, 100 regions, .5 s cooperative build deadline. Numerical calls are not hard-preemptible.

## Map-model boundary (approved revised contract)

The adapter uses the exact selected backend: global ESDF isStaticTraversable first, static PCD isStaticTraversable second, otherwise local SDF isTraversable. It changes no margins, vertical samples, producer, unknown policy or backend selection. Static PCD known masks remain floor-hit dilation/enclosed-region inference, not observed-free certificates. Local optimistic unknown distance values retain existing semantics. Global ESDF is supported without adding an observation requirement.

All three current queries are floor-indexed, piecewise constant in XY on their native aligned grid; local getDistance(position) does not interpolate. A center identifies the complete bin, not a point-obstacle approximation. Rejected bins become full convex obstacle cells, certified by separating planes. Vertical sample positions are constant per query, preserving legacy sampled body checks rather than claiming whole-height observation. If a future backend interpolates continuously this abstraction must be reviewed; finite point samples would not certify that interpolation. Cropping is aligned to backend origin/resolution, finite and bounded; exterior remains excluded by corridor bounds. Closed obstacle cells and metric clearance are conservative at bin boundaries.

Regression uses actual SDF initialization, filtered point-cloud ray fusion, inflation and ESDF update with default .15 m filter, slice height zero and optimistic policy. It compares every adapter bin with the legacy query, verifies filtered input remains unknown, observed obstacles reject, and unknown-only cells retain the original optimistic decision. Synthetic static/ESDF fixtures additionally verify selected-backend precedence and known-policy flags.

## Approved original-edge segment revision (2026-09-07)

The runtime now walks original A* edges without shortcuts, subdividing at a configurable maximum segment length (default 1 m). Each restrictive solve protects both endpoints with the metric clearance norm included in its constraints; final normalized planes certify both endpoints and whole blocked cells. MVIE-only certified roundoff handling is unchanged.

The old min_progress, overlap_depth and overlap_area configuration is removed. Adjacent regions require a local-frame witness with at least 1e-6 m slack (100 times the 1e-8 m vertex feasibility tolerance), rather than a physical-sized disk or fixed area. A failed direct overlap inserts the same FIRI at the shared endpoint and checks both joins; failure clears all output. Connection regions count toward the global region/time budget. No footprint or LFPC guarantee follows.

Snapshot v2 stores max_segment_length. v1 readers explicitly consume and discard historical geometric-only progress/depth/area fields; the new segment length defaults to 1 m, never reinterpreting area as length. Historical failure diagnostics remain intact.

Historical original-edge verification (before pruning and angular separation): 13 geometry, 5 snapshot and 6 connector/MVIE-roundoff tests passed. All three exact historical fixtures require and achieve feasibility, each with 81 original edges and 81 regions. The historical closest-pair SLSQP initialization is superseded by the finite angular separator described above; it is not a runtime contract. Diagnostics now include endpoint constraint violations. Dense original paths consume one region per edge at minimum: no coalescing or cap increase was added. A 500-edge synthetic path explicitly fails the default 100-region budget (and passes with a test-only 600 cap). Connector tests exercise point-touch repair with same-FIRI inflation, thin-join rejection, occupied connector and expired deadline. Full catkin integration and navigation retest remain pending.
